## Paper / topic

Add the title, link, authors, venue, and the question you wanted to answer by reading it.

## Key idea

Summarize the main contribution in your own words.

## Method

Record the dataset, setup, baselines, evaluation metrics, or technical approach that matters for your work.

## What I learned

Write the concepts, methods, or results that are relevant to your thesis.

## My take

Record limitations, open questions, disagreements, and ideas for follow-up experiments.

## Thesis relevance

Explain in a few lines whether this paper changes, supports, or narrows your thesis direction.
